What is Pythe?
Pythe is an AI cofounder with a team of AI agents. You describe what you're building, Pythe writes up the company, researches the market and builds a ranked, stage-aware roadmap. Pytheus, the AI cofounder, hands tasks to six agents (researcher, writer, engineer, analyst, outreach and operator), each on its own cloud computer, and nothing goes live without the founder's approval. Daiwik Khedekar co-founded Pythe with Abhiram Kakarla and is its CEO.
