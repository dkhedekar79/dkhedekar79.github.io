## how it works
1. tell pythe what youre building. a link, a repo, a few notes, whatever
2. it writes up your company and researches the market
3. you get a ranked, stage-aware roadmap
4. pytheus (the ai cofounder) hands the tasks to six agents: researcher, writer, engineer, analyst, outreach and operator. each one gets its own cloud computer
5. nothing leaves pythe without your yes
